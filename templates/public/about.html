About Code Academy

Learn. Build. Innovate.

CodeAcademy is a technology learning platform created by SEA TECHNOLOGIES MEDIA to make quality tech education simple, practical, and accessible to everyone.

Whether you're taking your first step into programming or building on your existing knowledge, CodeAcademy is designed to guide you through every stage of your learning journey with structured lessons, practical projects, and real-world examples.

Our goal is not just to teach programming languages but to help learners develop the confidence, discipline, and problem solving mindset needed to succeed in today's digital world.

We believe that technology creates opportunities, and everyone deserves the chance to learn, build, and grow regardless of their background or previous experience.

🎯

Meet the Founder

Emmanuel Adedayo

Founder, SEA TECHNOLOGIES MEDIA

Founder & Lead Instructor, CodeAcademy

Technology has always fascinated me not just because of what it can do, but because of the opportunities it creates for people willing to learn.

As I continued my journey in software engineering, I noticed that many aspiring developers struggled to find learning resources that were simple, practical, and easy to understand. Some had the passion to learn but didn't know where to begin, while others gave up because the learning process felt overwhelming.

That inspired me to create CodeAcademy.

My goal is to make learning technology less intimidating and more practical by breaking complex concepts into simple, structured lessons that anyone can understand and apply.

Every lesson on this platform is created with the learner in mind. I believe education should be clear, engaging, and focused on helping students build real skills not just complete tutorials.

Beyond CodeAcademy, I founded SEA TECHNOLOGIES MEDIA to promote innovation, creativity, and technology-driven solutions that positively impact individuals and communities.

Whether you're writing your first line of code or working on your first project, I'm excited to be part of your journey.

Thank you for choosing CodeAcademy.

I look forward to seeing what you'll build.
